Stage 1 • Level 1.3: Fiber Optic Media & Transceivers (Cisco NetAcad ITN Deep-Dive)

Welcome to OSI Layer 1: Optical Infrastructure. Unlike copper, fiber optic cabling transmits data via light pulses, rendering networks immune to RFI and EMI while supporting much higher bandwidths over extended distances.

1. Multimode Fiber (MMF)

• Core Profile: Larger core (usually 50/62.5 microns).
• Light Source: LEDs.
• Distance & Application: Shorter distances (up to 550m at 10Gbps). Ideal for LAN backbones and data center racks.

2. Single-Mode Fiber (SMF)

• Core Profile: Extremely narrow core (9 microns).
• Light Source: Laser diodes.
• Distance & Application: Long distances (up to 100km+). Used for enterprise campus backbones and service provider WANs.

3. Connectors (LC vs. SC)

• LC (Lucent/Little Connector): Small-form factor duplex connector utilizing a push-pull latch design; widely used with modern SFP modules.
• SC (Subscriber/Square Connector): A larger snap-in connector utilizing a push-pull locking mechanism.

4. SFP Transceiver Modules

• Hot-Swappable: Allows network engineers to swap modules without powering down switches or routers.
• Media Flexibility: Enables a single modular port to accept copper (RJ-45) or fiber (LC variants like 1000BASE-SX/LX) adapters.
